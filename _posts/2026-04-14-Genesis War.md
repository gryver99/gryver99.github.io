## Genesis War: A Grand Strategy Saga Reborn

The legendary SRPG series that defined a generation is back. Genesis War, developed by Meerkat Games and published by LINE Games, has officially begun its global rollout. This title serves as a modern reimagining of The War of Genesis 2, bringing the epic conflict of Antaria to a worldwide audience with stunning 3D graphics and deep tactical gameplay.

Strategic Brilliance in Every Turn
At its core, Genesis War is a tactical RPG that rewards precision and planning. Players must navigate a complex grid-based battlefield where elemental advantages and terrain mechanics can turn the tide of any encounter. The game features:

Over 70 Unique Heroes: Collect iconic characters like Princess Iyolin and G.S., each with full voice acting and spectacular ultimate abilities.

Ancient Machina: Control colossally powerful war machines that serve as tactical trump cards during boss raids and PvP skirmishes.

Immersive Storytelling: Follow a narrative-driven campaign that explores the war between the Silver Arrow and the Geysir Empire, complete with cinematic cutscenes.

High-Fidelity Experience
Moving away from its 2D roots, Genesis War utilizes a modern engine to deliver high-quality 3D visuals. From the "Blizzard Storm" skill effects to the intricate designs of the airships, the game is a visual treat for mobile gamers.

Launch and Rewards
With the Global Soft Launch starting today in regions like Canada and Australia, and the full global release scheduled for April 28, players can participate in pre-registration events to secure Legendary-tier gear and a Hero Selection Chest. Whether you are a fan of the original '90s classic or a newcomer to the genre, Genesis War is set to be one of the most significant SRPG releases of 2026.
