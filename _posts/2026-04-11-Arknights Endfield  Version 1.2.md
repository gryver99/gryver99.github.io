## Arknights: Endfield – Version 1.2 "At the Wake of Spring" Comprehensive Overview

The highly anticipated **Version 1.2 update, "At the Wake of Spring,"** for *Arknights: Endfield* is officially scheduled for release on **April 17, 2026**. This expansion serves as the climactic finale to the Wuling narrative arc, pushing the boundaries of both the game's tactical combat and its unique factory-building systems. From high-stakes narrative developments to significant technical optimizations, this update represents the largest content drop since the global launch.

### 1. Narrative Finale and New Explorations
The update continues the Wuling storyline following the mysterious events involving Ardashir. Players, acting as the Endministrator, are summoned by the city's leadership to face a growing crisis.
* **The Battle Against Nefarith:** For the first time, players will engage in a direct, multi-phase boss encounter against Nefarith, requiring precise elemental coordination and environmental awareness.
* **New Zones:** Two major areas have been added to the world map. The **Marker Stone** zone features a stunning visual style inspired by traditional ink-wash paintings, while the **Test Area** introduces rescue missions centered around recovering Core Modules to push back the encroaching Erosion Tide.

### 2. New Operator: Zhuang Fangyi
The roster expands with the introduction of **Zhuang Fangyi**, a 6-star Operator and the game's first dedicated **Electric hypercarry**. 
* **Role & Combat:** As the Viceroy of Wuling, she utilizes a sophisticated blend of swordplay and high-frequency Electric Arts. Her kit is designed to exploit elemental reactions, filling a crucial gap in current endgame team compositions.
* **New Banner System:** Version 1.2 introduces the **"Fest of Brilliance"** banner type. This new system includes a limited character retention mechanic, allowing players to pull for previously featured 6-star operators as off-rate units with a shared pity system, making it easier for new players to catch up.

### 3. AIC Industrial Expansion and Gameplay Systems
The core "factory" gameplay of Endfield receives a massive upgrade to support endgame progression.
* **Acid Production & Hetonite:** The update introduces the **Acid Production Line** and the **Purification Node** facility. These allow players to process complex chemicals to manufacture **Hetonite Gearing Components**, a new material required for crafting Level 70 legendary equipment.
* **New Outpost:** A third strategic outpost can now be established in the Wuling region, increasing the global power grid capacity and resource extraction limits.

### 4. Quality of Life (QoL) and Rewards
Gryphline has implemented several community-requested optimizations to streamline the daily experience:
* **One-Click Logistics:** Players can now collect resources and manage production from the Dijiang interface with a single button.
* **Batch Gear Assembly:** The inventory management system now supports mass-crafting and recycling of gear pieces.
* **Generous Launch Rewards:** To celebrate "At the Wake of Spring," all players will receive **1000 Oryberyls** and a total of **10 Limited Headhunting Permits** via login bonuses. Additionally, a "Free Monthly Pass" event will be active, stacking with existing subscriptions to provide double daily rewards for a limited time.

With the tease of the upcoming **Contingency Contract (CC)** mode and new two-operator combat mechanics in development, *Arknights: Endfield* continues to evolve as a premier cross-platform RPG experience.
