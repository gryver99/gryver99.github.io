## Conquer the Skies: Game of Thrones: Dragonfire Enters Global Beta

Following an initial testing phase, **Warner Bros. Games** has officially transitioned **Game of Thrones: Dragonfire** into an expanded **Global Technical Beta**. This phase allows players to experience the brutal political intrigue and high-flying warfare of Westeros 172 years before the fall of the Iron Throne. 

Currently, the game is live and playable in specific **Soft Launch regions**, including **Canada, Australia, Malaysia, Indonesia, and the Philippines**. Players in these territories can download the client directly, while the rest of the world can now sign up for invitation-based beta waves ahead of the Summer 2026 global launch.

### 1. The "Wings of War" Update
The current Beta build introduces the **Aerial Combat Engine (ACE)**. Unlike previous titles in the franchise, *Dragonfire* allows players to manually trigger "Dragon Arts" during real-time sieges. 
* **Dynamic Firebreath:** Players must now account for wind speed and altitude when deploying dragonfire against enemy phalanges.
* **Custom Hatchery:** The Beta unlocks the **Valyrian Heritage** system, where players can cross-breed dragon traits to focus on either speed or raw destructive power.

### 2. Regional Availability and Access
While the full global release is slated for later this year, the current Technical Beta is being used to stress-test regional servers. 
* **Active Regions:** Players with accounts based in **Canada** and **Australia** have full access to the in-game shop, while players in **Southeast Asia (Malaysia, Indonesia, Philippines)** are participating in the stability test.
* **Global Sign-ups:** For those outside these regions, Warner Bros. has opened a "Beta Portal" where users can link their WB Games Account to receive access keys for the upcoming European and North American test windows.

### 3. Deepening the Social Strategy
At the heart of the Beta is the expanded **Great House System**. Players are no longer just solitary lords; they must pledge fealty to larger player-run alliances to survive the "Dance of Dragons" events.
* **The Council of Lords:** High-ranking members of a House can now vote on "Edicts" that provide resource buffs to the entire alliance.
* **Territorial Erosion:** A new mechanic where borders shift dynamically based on successful dragon strikes, forcing alliances to maintain active patrols along their frontiers.

### Technical Performance
Warner Bros. Games Boston has utilized this Beta to showcase significant graphical improvements. Utilizing the latest mobile rendering pipelines, the game features dynamic weather that directly impacts troop movement and dragon visibility. Testers who reach Level 15 during this phase will be awarded the exclusive **"First Rider"** cosmetic skin, which will carry over to their permanent account at launch.
