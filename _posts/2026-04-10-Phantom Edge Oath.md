## Phantom Edge: Oath – A Masterclass in High-Octane Shadow Combat

The mobile Action RPG landscape has a new titan. Phantom Edge: Oath, released globally on April 2, 2026, by X1 Global, is a dark fantasy epic that prioritizes skill-based combat and breathtaking aesthetics. Set in a world inspired by Eastern folklore and gothic "shadow" stylings, the game tasks players with becoming a Phantom Warrior—a protector sworn to an ancient oath to keep the balance between the mortal realm and the void.

Combat Without Compromise
Unlike many mobile titles that rely heavily on "Auto-play" mechanics, Phantom Edge: Oath emphasizes manual precision. The Dynamic Combo System allows players to chain aerial attacks, parries, and "Phantom Skills" together for devastating results. Every encounter feels weighty and deliberate, rewarding players who master dodge timing and enemy patterns.

Key Features of the Oath:
Unique Class System: Choose from several "Edges," each representing a different weapon style—from the swift dual blades of the Shadow Stalker to the heavy, soul-crushing Greatsword of the Warden.

Breathtaking Visuals: Powered by the latest mobile rendering tech, the game features high-fidelity character models and atmospheric environments that rival console titles.

The "Phantom Mode": Unleash your inner demon by activating your Oath. This temporary transformation boosts your stats and changes your skill set, turning the tide in difficult boss battles.

Competitive PvP & Co-op: Test your skills in 1v1 duels or team up with friends to tackle massive "Void Raids" for legendary loot.

A Fair-to-Play Ecosystem
X1 Global has launched the game with a clear focus on long-term sustainability. While it features a hero and gear collection system, much of the rarest equipment can be earned through Hardcore Challenges and seasonal events. This "skill-over-wallet" approach has already earned the game a dedicated following within the ARPG community.

If you are looking for a game that combines the stylish action of Devil May Cry with the deep progression of modern RPGs, Phantom Edge: Oath is a must-download on Android and iOS.
