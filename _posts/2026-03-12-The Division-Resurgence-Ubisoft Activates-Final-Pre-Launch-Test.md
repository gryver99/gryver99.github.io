## The Division Resurgence: Ubisoft Activates Final Pre-Launch Test Ahead of March 31 Global Release

The wait is almost over for mobile agents. Ubisoft has officially announced that Tom Clancy’s The Division Resurgence will launch globally on March 31, 2026. To ensure a seamless deployment, the developers have activated the final pre-launch technical test phase, allowing a selected group of "Insiders" and pre-registered users to stress-test the servers one last time.

Launch Details & Roadmap
Global Release Date: March 31, 2026.

Platforms: iOS and Android.

Model: Free-to-Play Third-Person RPG Shooter.

Setting: A massive, open-world New York City during the initial "Green Poison" outbreak.

The Final Test Phase: What’s New?
This final "Activation Phase" is focusing on the game’s core MMO infrastructure. Players currently in the test can experience:

Server Stability at Scale: Testing the regional data centers for the upcoming global influx of players.

Final PvP Tuning: Balancing passes for the Dark Zone and Conflict modes based on data from the February regional betas.

Technical Optimization: Final performance tweaks for mid-range devices to ensure the "PC-class" visuals remain stable across a wide array of smartphones.

Celebrating 10 Years of The Division
The launch of Resurgence coincides with the 10th Anniversary of the franchise (which debuted in March 2016). To celebrate, Ubisoft is offering significant cross-game rewards:

NYC Firefighter Cosmetic Set: Exclusive for those who pre-register before the March 31 launch.

Pure Gold Weapon Skins: Available to all early adopters.

The Division 2 Integration: Players who log into Resurgence using their Ubisoft Connect account will automatically unlock exclusive rewards in the console/PC version of The Division 2.

The Vision: A True AAA Mobile Experience
Unlike many mobile spin-offs, Resurgence is a standalone chapter with its own unique storyline that runs parallel to the events of the first game. Built on Unreal Engine 4, it features:

Full Cooperative Campaign: Playable solo or in co-op with up to 3 friends.

Authentic Tactical Combat: The signature cover-based shooting and gadget-driven gameplay of the mainline series.

Deep RPG Progression: A revamped loot system designed for shorter, mobile-friendly sessions without sacrificing complexity.
