## Tomb Raider I-III Remastered Just Dropped on Android, ios and switch

Lara Croft is officially entering her mobile era. After dominating PC and consoles back in 2024, Tomb Raider I-III Remastered just surprise-dropped on Android and iOS (alongside the shiny new Switch 2!) this week.

If you're ready to raid some tombs, lock the butler in the freezer, and dodge T-Rexes on your commute, here is everything you need to know about this massive mobile release.

🎮 The Ultimate 90s Glow-Up
Let’s get one thing straight: this isn't some cheap, watered-down mobile spin-off packed with ads. Aspyr and Crystal Dynamics brought the exact same critically acclaimed remaster straight to your phone. You get the iconic classic gameplay, but with a massive graphical glow-up, modernized controls, and an intuitive new UI built specifically for touchscreens. (Oh, and yes—it has full controller support if you want to connect a Backbone or Bluetooth pad!)

🔥 What’s New? The "Challenge Mode" Update
To celebrate the mobile launch, the devs dropped a massive, totally free patch that seriously changes the game. Here is what is fresh for this release:

The All-New Challenge Mode: Think you're a pro? You can now completely customize your runs. Tweak enemy damage, health, and bonuses to create your own brutal modifiers. The harder the rating, the more loot you unlock.

10 New Outfits (with Buffs!): Fashion meets function. Unlocking these new fits doesn't just make Lara look cool; they actually give you upgraded abilities to run faster, hit harder, and dive deeper.

15 Brand New Achievements: Flex your skills and boost your mobile gaming trophy cabinet.

💸 The Damage: Price & Free Trial
Mobile games can be sketchy with microtransactions, but Aspyr is keeping it incredibly real with a premium, one-time purchase.

The Price: Right now, it's sitting at a limited-time launch discount of $14.99 (50% off the standard $29.99 price tag).

Try Before You Buy: Not sure if your phone can handle it, or if classic exploration is your vibe? You can download the app and play the first level of every single game completely for FREE before dropping any cash.

📱 Should You Cop It?
If you're a fan of action-adventure games or just want a premium, no-BS, zero-microtransaction banger to play offline, this is an absolute must-play. It's the definitive way to experience gaming royalty right from your pocket.
