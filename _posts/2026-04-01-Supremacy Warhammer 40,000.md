A War of Weeks, Not Minutes
Unlike traditional RTS games, Supremacy: Warhammer 40,000 is designed for the patient tactician. Players join maps supporting either 30 or 64 players, where every troop movement, resource shipment, and fortification takes real-world time. Success isn't just about clicking fast; it’s about logistics, diplomacy, and long-term planning.

Set on the vital sentinel world of Vigilus, players must navigate the treacherous Nachmund Gauntlet. You aren't just fighting a battle; you are managing a planetary war effort where a single betrayal or a midnight offensive can shift the balance of power across the entire sector.

Playable Factions
At launch, the game features four iconic factions, each balanced for the long-term strategic format while maintaining their unique lore-friendly flavor:

Space Marines: The elite tip of the spear, focusing on high-quality units and tactical superiority.

Astra Militarum (Imperial Guard): A war machine fueled by endless ranks of guardsmen and devastating artillery.

Chaos Space Marines: Master of terror and dark gifts, bringing ruin to the followers of the Corpse-Emperor.

Orks: A green tide that thrives on constant conflict and overwhelming numbers.

Key Features
Persistent Living Maps: Campaigns run 24/7. Your empire continues to produce resources and move troops even while you are offline.

Deep Economic Management: Control vital forge worlds and hive cities to fuel your war machine. Managing your supply lines is as important as the combat itself.

Dynamic Diplomacy: Form "War Pacts" with other players to crush common enemies, or backstab your allies when the time is right to claim total supremacy.

Authentic Lore: From the gothic architecture of the cities to the unit descriptions, the game is steeped in the official Warhammer 40,000 aesthetic and narrative.
