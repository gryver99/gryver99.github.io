## Invincible: Guarding the Globe Update 3.0 Is Live: Ragnar’s Claw and Chapter 20 Have Arrived


The Global Defense Agency (GDA) has just issued a massive clearance for all field agents. Ubisoft has officially released Update 3.0 for Invincible: Guarding the Globe, and it’s a heavy-hitter. Whether you're a seasoned Viltrumite hunter or a new recruit, this patch brings substantial gear upgrades and the long-awaited continuation of the main storyline.

Here is everything you need to know about the latest drop.

New Artifact: Ragnar’s Claw
The highlight of Update 3.0 is the introduction of Ragnar’s Claw, a formidable new artifact that promises to shift the meta. Inspired by the savage beasts capable of piercing Viltrumite skin, this artifact is designed for high-impact damage.

Primary Effect: Expect a significant boost to your team's offensive capabilities, specifically tailored for shredding through high-defense targets.

Strategy Tip: Pair this with high-attack speed heroes to maximize the proc rate and tear through enemy frontlines like they’re made of paper.

The Story Continues: Season 2, Chapter 20
The narrative is heating up as players can now jump into Chapter 20 of Season 2. Cecil Stedman isn’t known for giving easy assignments, and this chapter pushes the GDA squad to their limits.

As the multiverse stakes grow higher, Chapter 20 promises new challenges and narrative twists that tie directly into the darker themes of the Invincible universe. If your team isn't at peak performance, now is the time to start those hero rank-ups.

Quality of Life & Balancing Changes
Beyond the new toys, Ubisoft has refined the gameplay experience with several much-needed updates:

UI Overhaul: The Artifacts and Totems menus have been unified for a cleaner, more intuitive experience.

Hero Buffs: Capevincible has received a significant power boost across his entire kit, increasing his damage output and life leech.

Fixes: General Kregg and Robot (Totem) summons now correctly reflect the character's real stats, including equipment.

Ads Removed: In a move that will please many, ads have been disabled to ensure a smoother, more immersive flow.

Agent's Note: Keep an eye on the recruitment shop; Ubisoft has teased that two new heroes are releasing soon to join the ranks.
