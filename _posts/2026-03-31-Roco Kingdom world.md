Developed by Tencent Games’ Magic Square Studio, Roco Kingdom: World is a massive open-world MMORPG that brilliantly blends deep creature-collecting mechanics with breathtaking exploration. Built upon the legacy of the iconic 2010 web game franchise, this highly anticipated title brings the beloved universe into the modern gaming era, drawing exciting comparisons to giants like Pokémon and Genshin Impact.

Players step into the role of a freshman at the Magic Academy, embarking on an epic journey to explore diverse biomes, solve elemental puzzles, and forge bonds with hundreds of magical creatures known as "Rocos."

Core Gameplay & Key Features:

Expansive Open-World Exploration: Roam freely across a visually stunning, anime-inspired 3D world filled with distinct regions, hidden secrets, and dynamic weather systems. You can even ride your Rocos across land, sea, and sky.

True Monster Catching (No Gacha): Unlike many modern mobile RPGs, Roco Kingdom: World steps away from the "pet gacha" system. Players can discover, battle, and freely catch over 400 unique Rocos directly in the wild, making for a truly rewarding collection experience.

Strategic Turn-Based Combat: Engage in classic, tactical turn-based battles. Each Roco belongs to specific elemental types, requiring players to build balanced teams, utilize elemental weaknesses, and unleash spectacular magic attacks to secure victory.

Rich Lifestyle & Social Elements: Beyond battling, the game offers a relaxing second-life experience. Players can enjoy farming, hatching eggs, customizing their outfits, and interacting with a vibrant community of other players in a shared online world.

Seamless Cross-Platform Play: Designed for accessibility, the game supports full cross-play and data synchronization across PC, Android, and iOS devices.

Release Status: Roco Kingdom: World officially launched in mainland China on March 26, 2026, quickly dominating the charts and garnering millions of players. While a global release date has yet to be officially announced, international anticipation is at an all-time high as Tencent prepares to bring this next-generation monster-tamer to the western market
