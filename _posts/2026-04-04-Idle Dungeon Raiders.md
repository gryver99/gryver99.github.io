Prepare to dive into the depths of the underworld without breaking a sweat. Idle Dungeon Raiders is the latest entry into the AFK RPG genre, combining tactical hero management with the satisfying progression of a classic dungeon crawler—all while you’re away from your screen.

Currently in its Beta phase, this title is inviting players to help shape the future of the realm. Whether you are a hardcore strategist or a casual looter, here is what you need to know about the journey ahead.

Key Features
Zero-Effort Progression: Your raiders never sleep. Even when the app is closed, your team continues to battle monsters, gain experience, and collect epic loot.

Strategic Team Building: Choose from a diverse roster of heroes, each with unique abilities and skill trees. Synergize your party to overcome punishing boss encounters.

Endless Dungeon Depth: From crumbling ruins to volcanic caverns, explore ever-changing environments filled with hidden treasures and increasingly dangerous foes.

Loot & Crafting System: Equip your raiders with legendary gear and utilize a deep crafting system to forge the ultimate weapons of war.

Beta Access Notice
Please Note: Idle Dungeon Raiders is currently in Open Beta. As the game is still in active development, players may encounter occasional bugs, balance changes, and frequent content updates. Your feedback during this period is crucial for the developers to refine the gameplay experience before the official global launch.
