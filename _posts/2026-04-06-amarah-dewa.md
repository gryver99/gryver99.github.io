## Challenge the Gods

In the Nine Realms, the pantheon rules with absolute power and indifferent cruelty. Their insatiable greed has unleashed the **Celestial Wrath**, a cataclysm that tears the skies asunder and devours the earth.

From this divine disaster, you are born: a mortal tempered by chaos, infused with a shattered power — the first of the **Godslayers**. As the deities pour their fury upon the world, your task is to rally the rebels and march on Asgard. Not to plead for mercy, but to topple their thrones and crown yourself a god atop their ruins.

The fate of the world and the dawn of a new era depend on your strength and your ambition.

## Key Features

- 🔥 **NORSE FANTASY OPEN WORLD** Traverse mythical realms beneath the shadow of the Great World Tree. Explore vast, mysterious maps — from sacred Asgard to the frozen wastes of Niflheim.

- ⚔️ **DEFY THE DIVINE** Only true warriors dare to face the gods. Battle Supreme Deities alone or in a squad. Push your limits in epic encounters.

- 🛡️ **LEAD THE GLOBAL WAR** Engage in colossal, real-time divine wars on global servers. Rally your legion and conquer sacred strongholds across massive maps.

- 💎 **JOIN THE ROUND TABLE** Forge alliances, fight, trade, and grow alongside players from around the world. Climb the leaderboards and unlock shared perks.

- 📱 **AAA QUALITY ON MOBILE** Console-grade 3D graphics meet the scale of classic isometric RPGs. Fluid and responsive PvP and Co-op combat.

- 🎁 **BOOSTED DROP RATES** Divine gear can drop anywhere: dungeons, monsters, or events. Every battle is an opportunity to claim legendary loot.
