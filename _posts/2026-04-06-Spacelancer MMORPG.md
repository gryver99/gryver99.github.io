Sci-fi enthusiasts and space sim veterans have a new frontier to conquer. Developed by Haupt Game Productions, Spacelancer MMORPG is a top-down, open-universe sandbox designed to bring the scale of classic PC space sims to mobile devices. Shifting away from linear gameplay, this title focuses heavily on player freedom, dynamic economies, and intense space combat.

Gameplay: Shape Your Own Destiny
In Spacelancer, you aren’t bound by a pre-written story or fixed character classes. Instead, the game operates on an open skill and ship system. Your role on the battlefield—whether you want to be a heavy-armored Tank, a high-damage DPS, or a fleet-healing Support—is dictated entirely by the ship you fly and the modules you install.

Exploration & Navigation: Travel through a massive grid of sectors connected by jumpgates and unpredictable wormholes.

Action-Packed Combat: Master a top-down combat loop using primary weapons, secondary missiles, energy shields, and tactical special abilities.

A Living Economy: Mine rich asteroid fields, salvage derelict shipwrecks, and craft high-tier gear. Players can even build their own space stations and automated resource extractors to claim territory and secure rare materials.
