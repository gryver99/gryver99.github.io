## Sumo Digital and Arm Partner to Deliver "PC-Class" Graphics via Neural Technology

The boundary between high-end desktop visuals and handheld gaming is officially collapsing. Sumo Digital, one of the UK’s leading AAA developers, has announced a landmark collaboration with chip giant Arm to implement and test next-generation Neural Graphics Technology on mobile devices.

The Collaboration: Bridging the AAA Gap
Announced in March 2026, this partnership aims to bring "PC-class" gaming experiences to smartphones by leveraging Arm’s latest neural accelerators. Sumo Digital is among the first major studios to technically evaluate these AI-driven graphics techniques within a production-quality game environment, moving beyond theoretical research into real-world application.

Key Technical Breakthroughs
According to technical briefings from both Sumo Digital and Arm, the implementation of neural technology will offer:

Up to 50% GPU Workload Savings: By offloading complex graphical calculations to dedicated neural hardware, the main GPU can focus on raw performance.

2x Frame Rate Increase: Through AI-powered frame interpolation and upscaling (similar to DLSS or FSR on PC), mobile games can achieve smoother motion without overheating the device.

Ray Tracing & High Fidelity: The technology enables more sophisticated lighting, shadows, and reflections that were previously exclusive to high-end PCs and consoles.

Why It Matters for the Industry
Gary Dunn, Managing Director of Sumo Digital, described the move as a "paradigm shift for what is possible with video games on mobile." By integrating AI-driven graphics at the hardware level, Sumo Digital is positioning itself as a leader in the "Core Mobile" market. This allows for:

Simultaneous Multi-Platform Launches: Developers can build a single high-fidelity asset base and scale it down to mobile more efficiently.

Extended Battery Life: More efficient rendering means high-end games can run longer on standard mobile batteries without throttling performance.

Immersive Realism: Enhanced environments and character animations that match the visual quality of current-gen console titles.

Release Timeline
While Sumo Digital has not yet named the specific upcoming title utilizing this technology, the first wave of Arm GPUs equipped with these neural accelerators is expected to ship in flagship smartphones later in 2026. This aligns perfectly with the recent trend of publishers bringing full-scale AAA experiences to the mobile ecosystem.
