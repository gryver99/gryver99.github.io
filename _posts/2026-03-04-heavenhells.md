HEAVENHELLS is an anime-style RPG built around fast-paced, real-time tactical battles. You take on the role of a newly appointed Captain, suddenly thrust into becoming humanity’s last hope after the gates of Purgatory swing open and the Hellion invasion begins. To halt the advancing chaos, you must lead a squad of witches who are as adorable as they are incredibly powerful.


⚔️ Real-Time Tactical Combat
Build a squad of four WiTCHes and master each of their unique skills and synergies. By combining the girls' powers with your Commander abilities, you will dominate the Hellions on the battlefield.

Tactical Squad Building – Leverage unique skills and synergies to create the ultimate formation.

Real-Time Command – Lead your WiTCHes through corrupted battlefields with swift, strategic decision-making.

Explosive Action – Unleash devastating strikes and visually stunning abilities in fluid, satisfying combat.


💖 Your WiTCHes: Alive, Present, Unforgettable
The girls fighting by your side aren't just units to deploy—they are living characters rich with personality.

Breathtaking 3D Anime Graphics – Detailed models that faithfully recreate the original concept art.

Expressive Characters – Emotional reactions, eye contact, and natural animations: every interaction feels alive.

Bonds Beyond Battle – Share daily moments, secrets, and small joys. Discover sides of them that only you will ever see.


🎬 An Anime-Driven Story
Experience a cinematic adventure that blends emotion, mystery, and personal growth.

TV-Quality Production – A meticulously directed narrative, animated to mainstream anime standards.

A Journey of Trust and Discovery – Uncover the truth behind the disaster and forge unbreakable bonds with your WiTCHes.
