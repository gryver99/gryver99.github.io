## Last Furry: Survival - Fuel the Fury. Survive the Wild.

Last Furry: Survival is an upcoming mobile strategy and base-building survival game developed by StarUnion (the studio behind the massively successful The Ants: Underground Kingdom). Set in a grim, post-apocalyptic world, the game challenges players to lead a desperate group of survivors on the devastated planet "Furry," which has been swallowed by a deadly, mutating toxic fog.

As the Commander, players are tasked with rebuilding civilization from the ashes, managing vital resources, and defending their growing refuge from horrific mutated beasts.

🌍 The Premise: A World Choked by Fog

A cataclysmic conspiracy has released a toxic fog over the once-vibrant planet Furry, plunging it into apocalyptic chaos. While countless inhabitants perished, the deadly gas caused others to mutate into savage, bloodthirsty monsters. The remaining survivors are forced to endure an unforgiving wasteland plagued by extreme scarcity, unbreathable air, and constant danger. Your mission is to push back the toxic fog, purify the land, and reignite the spark of civilization.

⚙️ Key Gameplay Features

Shelter Building & Management: Transform ruins into a thriving refuge. Every decision matters as you manage food production, purify toxic air, establish medical facilities, and fortify your base against relentless outside threats.

Dynamic Survivor System: Your citizens aren't just numbers on a screen. They are living, breathing survivors with their own emotions, backgrounds, and needs. They will express joy when comfortable and despair when hungry. Players can interact with them through personal letters, resolve internal conflicts, and earn their loyalty.

High-Stakes Exploration: Send elite squads beyond the safety of the shelter and into the toxic unknown. These high-risk, high-reward expeditions allow you to scavenge vital resources and recover forgotten technologies, but you will have to battle hostile factions and mutated beasts along the way.

Hero Recruitment: Find and recruit unique heroes with specialized abilities. Build a balanced roster of fearsome warriors for combat, brilliant engineers to boost development, and mysterious figures with fate-altering powers to lead your squads.

Global Alliances: Survival is nearly impossible alone. Join forces with other Commanders worldwide to form powerful alliances, trade essential resources, share critical intelligence, and tackle formidable, evolving threats together.
