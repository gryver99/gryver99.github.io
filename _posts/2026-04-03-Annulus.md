Annulus is a highly anticipated, art-driven turn-based tactical RPG developed by Nirvana (with Hong Kong Indra Soft handling regional publishing). Heavily inspired by the gritty realism of medieval knightly warfare and drenched in a bleak dark fantasy aesthetic, the game officially launches its global servers on April 8, 2026, for PC (via Steam and Epic Games Store), Android, and iOS. 

Core Gameplay: Classic War Chess Meets Modern Brutality
At its core, Annulus is a tribute to classic grid-based "war chess" strategy games, heavily emphasizing positioning, unit management, and harsh counter-mechanics.

The Mercenary Captain: Players step into the iron-toed boots of a mercenary regiment leader on the war-torn continent of Noviseth. Survival and glory are your only metrics as you recruit, train, and maintain a diverse roster of hardened warriors.

Complex Rock-Paper-Scissors Tactics: Victory demands a deep understanding of the game's mutual restraint mechanisms. Different unit classes (infantry, cavalry, archers) and distinct weapon types must be combined perfectly to exploit enemy vulnerabilities.

Customization and Progression: Beyond moving units on a board, players must actively scavenge and cultivate resources. You can craft specialized weapons and etch dynamic inscriptions onto gear, allowing you to customize your squad's fighting style to match the shifting challenges of the campaign.

The Story: Breaking the Shackles of Fate
The narrative of Annulus is an epic tale of endless conflict. Set in a world trapped in brutal cycles of war, players are not shiny, flawless heroes saving the world. Instead, you are a leader fighting to break the literal and metaphorical "rules of fate" imposed on your men. Every battle is a fight against extinction, and the story features difficult moral and strategic choices that ripple across your campaign, dictating who lives and dies under your banner.

Visuals: Unreal Engine Powering a Grim Universe
One of the most striking elements of Annulus is its high-fidelity presentation, built on Unreal Engine.

Visceral Combat: The game steps away from the colorful, stylized anime aesthetics common in modern mobile RPGs. Instead, it offers a mature, desaturated, and intensely detailed look at medieval warfare.

Dynamic Battlefields: Mud-soaked fields, rain-slicked stone castles, and blood-stained armor tell a silent story of the world's harsh reality, making every skirmish feel heavy and impactful.
