## Cards of Myth: Command the Legends

Rewrite history and lead the gods into battle. Step into the arena of Cards of Myth, a next-generation strategic deck-builder where ancient legends, powerful deities, and mythical beasts clash for ultimate supremacy. Whether you’re a veteran of TCGs or a newcomer to the genre, Cards of Myth offers a deep, tactical experience optimized perfectly for your mobile device.

DEFEAT YOUR FOES WITH STRATEGY
In Cards of Myth, it’s not just about the power of your cards—it’s about how you use them. Master a unique lane-based combat system where positioning is the key to victory. Anticipate your opponent’s moves, sacrifice for power, and unleash devastating combos to shatter their defenses.

BUILD YOUR ULTIMATE PANTHEON
Collect and upgrade hundreds of cards featuring stunning hand-drawn art. From the thunderous halls of Olympus to the frozen realms of Norse mythology and the shifting sands of Egypt, create a deck that reflects your playstyle.

Synergy is Everything: Mix and match gods and creatures to discover game-changing abilities.

Deep Customization: Refine your deck as you face increasingly challenging trials.

KEY FEATURES:
⚔️ Fast-Paced Tactical Combat: Experience intense, turn-based battles designed for quick mobile sessions.

🎨 Stunning Visuals: Immerse yourself in a world of high-fidelity mythological art and fluid animations.

🃏 Collect & Evolve: Unlock rare cards and evolve your units to reach their true god-tier potential.

🌍 Global Strategy: Compete against players worldwide and climb the ranks of the mythic leaderboards.

EARLY ACCESS / SOFT LAUNCH
Be among the first to shape the future of the game! Cards of Myth is currently in Early Access. We are constantly updating the game with new cards, balance changes, and features based on player feedback. Join our community and help us build the ultimate mobile card game.
