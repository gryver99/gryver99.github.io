Retropolis: Rule The Streets is the latest multiplayer real-time strategy (RTS) sensation to hit the mobile market in 2026. Developed by Voidforge Studio (under Nathan Plouvier), this title pivots from the traditional adventure genre to deliver a gritty, high-stakes tactical experience centered on urban dominance and gang management.

The Premise: Reputation is Your Only Currency
Set in a sprawling, neon-drenched metropolis, players start as a nameless "nobody" with one goal: total control. Unlike static strategy games, Rule The Streets operates on a dynamic reputation system where every contract completed and every rival defeated increases your influence, unlocking higher-tier missions and deadlier allies.

Key Gameplay Features
Real-Time Multiplayer Warfare: Engage in live gang battles against players worldwide. Success depends on quick reflexes and even quicker strategic pivots.

Deep Card Crafting & Combo System: Victory isn’t just about numbers; it’s about your deck. Players must collect, craft, and upgrade ability cards to create devastating combos that can turn the tide of a turf war in seconds.

Gang Management: Recruit a diverse crew of specialists, each bringing unique buffs and tactical advantages to your organization.

Contract-Based Progression: Take on high-risk jobs to earn "Cash" and "Influence," allowing you to expand your territory block by block.

Persistent City Control: Join or form alliances to compete for seasonal dominance of the city’s major districts.
