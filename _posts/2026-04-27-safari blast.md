## Safari Blast: Unleash the Power of the Savannah!

Safari Blast is the latest entry from Small Barking Dog Studios, blending the addictive satisfaction of "tap-to-blast" puzzles with the strategic depth of turn-based hero battles. Set in a lush, vibrant world, the game invites players to explore diverse biomes—from sun-drenched savannahs to mysterious jungles—while solving increasingly complex grid-based challenges.

Strategic Puzzle Battles
Unlike traditional match-3 games, Safari Blast adds a layer of tactical combat. Every group of tiles you explode translates into damage against wild enemies. Success isn't just about clearing the board; it’s about timing your moves and exploiting elemental strengths to overcome formidable bosses.

Build Your Dream Team
The heart of the game lies in its Hero Collection system. Players can unlock and upgrade a roster of adorable yet powerful safari animals, each bringing a unique "Game-Changer" ability to the table:

Massive Blasts: Clear entire sections of the grid instantly.

Tile Manipulation: Change colors to set up legendary combos.

Tactical Shifts: Turn the tide of battle when you're cornered.

Why It Stands Out
With a focus on fair gameplay and a vibrant art style, Safari Blast is designed for both quick coffee-break sessions and longer strategic marathons. Hundreds of levels await, packed with hidden secrets and rare animals to discover. Whether you're a casual player or a puzzle veteran, the mix of explosive visuals and hero-based strategy offers a refreshing take on the mobile puzzle genre.
