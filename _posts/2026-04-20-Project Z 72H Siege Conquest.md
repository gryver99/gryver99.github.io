## Project Z: The Ultimate 72-Hour Survival Challenge
Unlike traditional base-builders that offer endless downtime, Project Z centers around a high-pressure 72-hour siege mechanic. Players are dropped into a post-apocalyptic wasteland where they must scavenge, recruit survivors, and fortify their defenses before the massive "Z-Wave" hits.

Strategic Depth and Tactical Combat
The core gameplay is divided into two distinct phases: Preparation and The Siege. During the preparation phase, you must manage limited resources to upgrade your walls, position automated turrets, and train specialized hero units. The game features a deep "Tech Tree" that allows for various playstyles, from heavy industrial fortification to stealthy, guerrilla-style tactics.

The 72-Hour Siege Mechanic
What sets this title apart is the real-time siege. Once the timer starts, the undead assault begins and persists for 72 hours. You must log in to manage your troops, repair breaches, and coordinate with alliance members to share resources. The persistent nature of the siege creates a sense of urgency rarely seen in mobile gaming—if your gate falls while you're offline, the consequences are devastating.

Key Features:
Persistent World: Your base exists in a shared map where alliances can thrive or betray one another.

Hero System: Collect and level up unique survivors with specialized abilities that can turn the tide of a losing battle.

Massive Scale: Experience thousands of on-screen zombies rendered with high-fidelity graphics optimized for modern mobile devices.

Global Conquest: Compete against other players for control over "Safe Zones," the only areas on the map providing rare end-game resources.

Whether you are a hardcore strategist or a survival enthusiast, Project Z: 72H Siege Conquest offers a punishing yet rewarding experience that tests your leadership under the ultimate ticking clock.
