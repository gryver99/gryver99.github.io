Prime Rush is a high-octane, next-generation mobile battle royale that blends traditional survival mechanics with tactical extraction gameplay. Developed by the Indian studio SuperGaming in strategic partnership with Spacecaps (the parent company of the prominent Brazilian esports organization LOUD), the game is designed with a "Brazil-first" approach, featuring localized themes, characters, and environments.

Game Overview & Mechanics
Unlike standard battle royales where the only goal is to be the last survivor, Prime Rush introduces a Dual Win Condition:

Survival: Outlast all other players to be the last one standing.

Cosmium Extraction: A unique "instant win" mechanic. During the match, a rare substance called Cosmium spawns on the map. The first player or squad to successfully capture and extract it wins the match immediately, regardless of how many opponents remain.

Key Features:

The Maré Map: A floating battleground inspired by authentic Brazilian landscapes and favelas, designed for fast-paced vertical combat and tactical positioning.

Brazilian Heroes: Players can choose from unique characters like Juliana, Raphael, and Rogério, each reflecting Brazilian culture and style.

Tactical Abilities: Each hero can be equipped with game-changing skills, such as:

Dead Eye: Spawns a legendary weapon where headshots restore ammo.

Shield Dome: Deploys a protective energy field for the squad.

Super Speed: Provides a temporary movement boost for flanking or escaping.

Hunter Instinct: Activates infrared vision to track enemies through walls.

Fast-Paced Action: Matches typically last 10–15 minutes, specifically optimized for mobile gaming sessions.


Release Date
Official Launch (Brazil): March 16, 2026.

Early Access Phase: Commenced in late January 2026 for a limited number of players to fine-tune performance and stability.

Available Regions & Platforms
As of its current rollout phase, the game's availability is prioritized as follows:

Initial Region: Brazil (available on both the Google Play Store and Apple App Store).

Planned Expansion: A wider release across Latin America (LATAM) is expected to follow shortly.

Global Status: While primarily focused on the Brazilian market, global pre-registrations have been sighted in other regions (such as India and Southeast Asia) through SuperGaming’s "SuperPlatform" tech stack, hinting at a broader international release later in 2026.
