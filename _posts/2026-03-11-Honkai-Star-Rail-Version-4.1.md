## Honkai: Star Rail Version 4.1: "Unraveled for Daybreak"

Prepare to witness the threads of fate tighten as the Trailblazer’s journey reaches a breathtaking turning point. In Version 4.1, "Unraveled for Daybreak," HoYoverse takes players back to the heart of the cosmos, where hidden truths about the Aeons begin to surface and old alliances are tested under the light of a dying star.

The Story: A New Chapter of the Astral Express
The Trailblaze Mission continues as the crew arrives at a new celestial landmark: The Weaving Garden of Seraphine. Once a sanctuary for followers of the Abundance, it has become a fractured reality where time flows backward. As the "Daybreak" approaches, players must navigate a labyrinth of memories to stop a mysterious faction from unravelling the fabric of the Imaginary Tree.

New Playable Characters
Version 4.1 introduces two highly anticipated units to the roster:

Valerius (5-Star | The Erudition: Quantum): A scholar from the Intelligentsia Guild who manipulates "String Dimensions." His kit revolves around applying Tension stacks to enemies, which explode into massive AoE damage when they attempt to take a turn.

Elara (4-Star | The Nihility: Imaginary): A rogue weaver who specializes in debuffing enemy speed. Her Ultimate, Sunset Requiem, traps foes in a temporal loop, delaying their actions while increasing the DoT (Damage over Time) they receive.

Innovative Gameplay Features
Tactical Domain: Resonance Echoes: A new permanent game mode where players must clear waves using "Echo Buffs"—special abilities that mimic the ultimates of characters not currently in the active party.

Relic Synthesis Overhaul: A "Quality of Life" update that allows players to prioritize specific sub-stats when using Self-Modeling Resin, making the grind for the perfect build more rewarding.

Expansion of the Simulated Universe: Introducing World 11, featuring the formidable new boss "The Weaver of Void," a multi-phase encounter that challenges even the most seasoned strategic minds.

Events and Rewards
To celebrate the launch of "Unraveled for Daybreak," the "Stellar Dawn" Log-in Event returns, offering players up to 10 Star Rail Special Passes. Additionally, the limited-time event “Chronicles of the Loom” will allow Trailblazers to earn a free 4-star Light Cone and exclusive chat bubbles by completing narrative-driven puzzles.
