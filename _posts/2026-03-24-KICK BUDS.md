KICK BUDS is the latest multiplayer sensation from Konami Digital Entertainment, blending the high-energy competitive spirit of soccer with the unpredictable, chaotic fun of a "party royale." Moving away from the hyper-realism of eFootball, Konami delivers a vibrant, physics-based survival experience where soccer moves are your primary weapon.

Game Overview
In KICK BUDS, you don’t just play a match; you fight to stay on the pitch. Players control highly customizable avatars in a series of fast-paced rounds featuring wacky rules and diverse environments. Whether you are dodging obstacles on a street court or outmaneuvering opponents in a stadium, the goal is simple: use your soccer skills to be the last one standing.

Core Gameplay Mechanics
The game features an intuitive control system designed for both casual players and competitive enthusiasts. Core actions include:

Dynamic Moves: Execute bicycle kicks, headers, and chip shots with simple taps and swipes.

Physics-Based Chaos: The ball and players interact in a "bouncy," unpredictable way, making every goal a potential "miracle shot."

Survival Elements: It’s not just about scoring; you must outlast opponents in free-for-all matches where the rules change constantly.

Key Game Modes
Party Royale: A classic battle royale format where players compete through three rounds of escalating difficulty. Only the most skilled (or luckiest) players reach the final to be crowned champion.

Soccer Royale: An intense team-based mode focusing on 3v3 tactical battles. Coordination and teamwork are essential to blast through the opponent's defenses in a chaotic three-team free-for-all.

Ranked & Casual Matches: Test your skills against the world in competitive rankings or enjoy a low-stakes game with friends.

Customization & Social Features
Express Yourself: A robust customization engine allows you to modify your avatar’s appearance, from outfits to unique celebrations.

Social Interaction: The game encourages sportsmanship (or friendly rivalry) with features like the "post-game handshake" and interactive emotes.
