## Mobile Legends: Bang Bang Kicks Off Season 40 with New Hero "Marcel" and the Shuffle Draw Event

The Land of Dawn is officially entering a new era. As of March 11, 2026, Mobile Legends: Bang Bang (MLBB) has launched Season 40: Decisive Moment, bringing a fresh wave of competitive energy and a brand-new mechanical challenge for Support mains: Marcel, the Soul Photographer.

New Hero: Marcel (Support/CC)
Marcel has officially stepped out of the Advanced Server and into Ranked play. Known as the Soul Photographer, he introduces a unique "Stasis" mechanic that can completely reset the momentum of a team fight.

Passive – Platinum Snap: Marcel doesn't build like a traditional tank; he converts extra HP into Hybrid Defense. His camera, Clemar, automatically snaps photos of nearby enemies, dealing True Damage and granting shields to Marcel and his closest ally.

Skill 1 – Framed Moment: A powerful AOE slow that immobilizes enemies after a short delay, perfect for setting up ganks.

Skill 2 – Tracking Shot: An agility tool that gives Marcel a massive movement speed burst when moving toward enemies, allowing him to reposition for the perfect shot.

Ultimate – Golden Hour: Marcel’s signature move. He creates a wide-angle zone for 3 seconds that puts all units (allies and enemies alike) into Stasis. While in Stasis, units are immobilized and immune to all damage—except from Marcel himself.

Pro Tip: Use Marcel’s Ultimate at Lord or Turtle pits to freeze the enemy Jungler right before they can use Retribution!


Shuffle Draw Event: Claim Your Epic Skin
Coinciding with the new season is the Shuffle Draw event. This limited-time event is a goldmine for players looking to bolster their skin collection without breaking the bank.

Guaranteed Rewards: By completing daily tasks and event missions, players can earn tokens to participate in draws.

The Big Prize: The event features a guaranteed permanent Epic-tier skin for players who stay active and complete the shuffle milestones.

Rare Resources: Beyond skins, players can collect rare fragments and exclusive battle effects to customize their profile for the new season.


Season 40 Rewards & What's Next
With the reset, players can now claim their S39 rewards and start the grind for the S40 First Top-Up skin, Esmeralda’s "Petal’s Razor." The month of March is looking stacked for MLBB fans. Keep an eye out for the Fanny "Galactic Starhawk" Legend Skin draw and the highly anticipated Naruto 2.0 Collaboration arriving later this month, featuring Minato Namikaze and Itachi Uchiha skins.
