Nekopara Sekai Connect (often abbreviated as Nekoconne) is the latest entry in the world-renowned "catgirl comedy" franchise. Developed by Good Smile Company in collaboration with NEKO WORKs, this title marks a significant shift for the series, moving from the traditional linear visual novel format to a free-to-play social RPG experience.

Story and Setting
Set in a near-future world where humans live alongside genetically engineered, AI-assisted catgirls (Nekos), the story follows the familiar setting of the patisserie La Soleil.

The narrative kicks off when the player is invited to the Neko Festival, a global competition where shop owners and their Nekos compete for popularity. Legend has it that the winner of the festival will be granted a single wish by the mysterious "Neko-creating AI."

Key Gameplay Features
Unlike previous volumes, Sekai Connect introduces several interactive systems:

Cafe Management: A sandbox-style mode where players can customize and manage their own shop to attract customers.

Character Collection (Gacha): Players can collect "Nekos from around the world." In addition to fan-favorites like Chocola and Vanilla, the game introduces brand-new original characters designed by series creator Sayori.

Semi-Auto Battles: Strategic but accessible card-based battles where players deploy their Neko team to win popularity contests.

Rhythm Elements: The game features exclusive music and rhythm mini-games, complete with high-quality 2D animations (Live2D).

Full Voice Acting: The main story and individual character episodes are fully voiced by a star-studded cast, including Nozomi Yamamoto (Fraise) and Yuki Yagi (Chocola).

Technical Details & Launch
Developer/Publisher: Good Smile Company / NEKO WORKs.

Mobile Release Date: April 14, 2026 (Global launch on iOS and Android).

PC Release Date: Scheduled for Fall 2026 via Steam.

Cross-Progression: The game supports account linking, allowing players to start on mobile in April and carry their progress over to the PC version later in the year.

Languages: Full support for English and Japanese at launch.

Why It Matters
Nekopara Sekai Connect represents the evolution of a franchise that has sold over 7 million copies worldwide. By integrating gacha mechanics and management simulation, it aims to provide a "live service" experience that keeps fans engaged long after the initial story ends.
