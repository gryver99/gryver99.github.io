If you’ve been waiting for a reason to fix your trainer’s "mid" outfit, this is it. We’re talking aesthetic rewards, fairy-core vibes, and challenges that are actually worth the walk.

It’s not just about the Pokémon anymore; it’s about the Fit Check. For the first time, Niantic is using Pokopia’s high-fidelity 3D assets to give our avatars a "Next-Gen" look.
