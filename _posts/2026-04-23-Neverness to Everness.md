## Global Announcement: Neverness to Everness Official China Launch

As of today, April 23, 2026, the supernatural urban landscape of Hethereau has officially opened its gates to players in China. This marks the first phase of the rollout for Neverness to Everness (NTE), allowing the local community to dive into a revolutionary open-world experience built on Unreal Engine 5. While the global audience awaits the worldwide release on April 29, the Chinese launch showcases the full scope of what Hotta Studio has achieved: a seamless blend of high-octane action, deep life-simulation, and an eerie supernatural mystery.

The World and Story Players take on the role of an Appraiser working for the Bureau of Anomaly Control, operating out of a mysterious antique shop. The setting, Hethereau, is a sprawling, neon-lit metropolis where "Anomalies"—paranormal phenomena ranging from sentient vending machines to reality-warping entities—are part of daily life. The narrative focuses on uncovering the truth behind these disturbances while balancing the mundane needs of city living.

Core Gameplay Mechanics * Esper-Driven Combat: NTE features a fast-paced, character-switching combat system. Players build a squad of "Espers," each possessing unique supernatural abilities. Mastery lies in synergy, where combining different powers triggers devastating elemental and gravitational reactions to neutralize threats.

Urban Sandbox & Life-Sim: Beyond the battles, the game offers unprecedented freedom. Players can purchase and customize luxury penthouses, manage their own businesses (like cafes or shops), and engage in social activities including mahjong, fishing, and street exploration.

Advanced Driving & Car Culture: A major pillar of the game is its deep vehicle system. With a massive catalog of cars, players can engage in professional-grade tuning and customization, modifying everything from performance parts to aesthetics. The driving experience is bolstered by high-speed racing and even "GTA-style" freedom within the city streets.

Technical Innovation and Collaborations Developed with Ray Tracing and Nanite technology, the game delivers stunning visuals, particularly on PC and PS5 (with confirmed PS5 Pro enhancements). The launch also features high-profile collaborations, including an in-car radio soundtrack featuring tracks from Persona 5 Royal and upcoming driveable Porsche models. This Chinese debut serves as the definitive preview for the global community, proving that the future of urban open-world RPGs has officially arrived.
