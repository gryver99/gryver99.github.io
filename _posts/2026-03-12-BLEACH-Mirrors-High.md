## BLEACH: Mirrors High – Everything We Know About Tite Kubo’s New Mobile Project 

The BLEACH franchise is entering a new era. Following the massive success of the Thousand-Year Blood War anime, Bandai Namco Entertainment has officially announced BLEACH: Mirrors High, a high-budget smartphone title set to launch globally in Summer 2026. 

The Unprecedented Involvement of Tite Kubo
What sets Mirrors High apart from previous mobile adaptations is the direct and intensive involvement of the original creator, Tite Kubo. During the official reveal, Kubo shared a personal message confirming his deep integration into the development process from day one:

"I have been involved from the kickoff meeting and worked on the title, scenario, and character designs. I got so excited working on them and ended up doing more than what was requested." — Tite Kubo

Key contributions by Kubo include:

Character Design: Dozens of new, original designs and "alternate" looks for existing characters.

Scenario & Lore: The story is partially connected to the main manga/anime timeline, offering new perspectives on the lore.

Art Direction: Kubo personally designed the game’s logo to ensure it matched his signature aesthetic.

Sound & Voice: He actively participated in voice actor auditions and recording sessions to ensure the characters sound exactly as he envisioned.

Gameplay & Narrative
While full gameplay footage is still pending a "Summer 2026" deep-dive reveal, official teasers categorize the title as an Action RPG. Rumors and leaks suggest a high-fidelity visual style similar to Bleach: Rebirth of Souls, optimized for high-end mobile devices.

The subtitle "Mirrors High" and the teaser visual suggest a narrative theme revolving around reflections or alternate versions of reality, allowing for "what-if" scenarios that fans have long requested.

Why 2026 is the Year of Bleach
The release of Mirrors High is strategically timed to coincide with the broadcast of the final part of the anime, BLEACH: Thousand-Year Blood War – The Calamity, which is also scheduled for July 2026. This cross-media synergy aims to provide fans with a definitive conclusion to the anime while offering an interactive, expanding experience via the mobile game.
