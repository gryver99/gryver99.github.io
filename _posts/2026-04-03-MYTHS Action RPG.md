MYTHS is a high-octane, isometric Action RPG (ARPG) developed by the Finnish studio Spectarium Games. Officially launched in early 2026, the game represents a bold evolution of the hack-and-slash genre, blending visceral, real-time combat with a rich tapestry of global mythologies. Designed with a "cross-platform first" philosophy, MYTHS offers a seamless experience whether playing solo on PC or engaging in 4-player co-op raids on mobile devices 

The Narrative: A War Between Realms
The world of MYTHS is teetering on the edge of the Ragnarok. The fires of Muspelheim are rising, and Chaos Portals have begun to fracture the boundaries between ancient pantheons. Players are thrust into the role of a chosen hero tasked with preventing the fire giant Surtr from consuming the World Tree.

The story doesn't stop at Norse myth; it expands across a "multiverse of legends," where the Mayan underworld of Xibalba and other ancient realms collide, forcing gods and mortals to forge unlikely alliances.

Gameplay: Visceral Combat & Precision Timing
Unlike many mobile-centric RPGs that rely on "auto-battle" mechanics, MYTHS emphasizes player skill and precision.

Tactical Action: The combat system is built around a responsive dodge and parry mechanic. Players must learn enemy patterns and time their strikes to trigger devastating elemental counters.

Iconic Heroes: Players don’t just meet legends; they become them. The roster includes iconic figures like Thor (Norse) and Chac Chel (Mayan), each featuring unique skill trees and playstyles—from heavy-hitting tanks to high-mobility mages.

Epic Boss Encounters: The game is defined by its screen-filling, multi-phase boss fights. From the bat-god Camazotz to the world-ending Surtr, these encounters require strategic coordination and high-tier gear management.

Co-op and Social Features
MYTHS shines in its 4-player cooperative mode. The game’s "Social Hub" allows players to form squads for challenging dungeon raids. The difficulty scales dynamically, rewarding teams that balance their roles—such as combining a defensive Vanguard with a glass-cannon caster to clear high-level "Chaos Rifts."

Innovation: Loot and The "Mintarium" Integration
One of the most unique aspects of MYTHS is its gear progression. Loot is not just a stat-boost; it is evolutionary. Finding legendary weapons changes the physical appearance and ability set of your hero, moving them from "mortal" to "divine" status.
