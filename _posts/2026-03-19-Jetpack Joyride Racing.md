## Barry Steakfries Returns in High-Octane Multiplayer Spin-off 

Halfbrick Studios has officially expanded the "Barryverse" with Jetpack Joyride Racing, a real-time multiplayer kart racer that moves away from the series’ endless-runner roots into the competitive world of high-speed racing. Following a successful soft launch in select regions, the game is set for a global Android rollout on March 19, 2026, with the iOS release following on April 10, 2026.

🎮 Gameplay Mechanics: Momentum and Mastery
Unlike traditional kart racers that rely on a simple "go" button, Jetpack Joyride Racing introduces a unique Skill-Based Momentum System.

Twin Jet Engines: Your vehicle’s thrusters are always on. To gain maximum speed, players must angle their jets against track walls—getting close enough to harness the thrust without "scraping" the surface, which causes significant speed loss.

Tactical Drifting: Success depends on mastering the drift mechanic to maintain speed through tight corners and complex laboratory-themed circuits.

Dynamic Track Zones: Races are defined by three distinct tactical zones:

Charge Zone (Green): Provides a rapid speed boost and recharges special abilities.

Slow Zone (Purple): Drastically reduces speed; must be avoided or navigated quickly.

Cutout Zone (Red): Temporarily disables engine control, forcing players to rely on existing momentum.

🚀 Key Features
6-Player Multiplayer: Compete in chaotic real-time matches against global rivals or engage in Party Mode for private lobbies with friends.

All-Star Roster: Play as iconic characters from the Halfbrick universe, including Barry Steakfries, Dan the Man, Josie, and Professor Brains.

Integrated Social Play: In a first for the studio, the game features built-in Discord Voice Chat, allowing teams to coordinate and trash-talk directly within the app without switching tools.

Halfbrick+ Cards: The game serves as the debut title for the "Halfbrick+ Cards" ecosystem. Players earn card packs through racing to unlock unique cosmetics, vehicle upgrades, and visual effects that persist across the entire Halfbrick+ game catalog.

📦 Ecosystem & Availability
The game operates under a Tiered Subscription Model via Halfbrick+, though it remains Free-to-Play at the base level:

Free Tier: Ad-supported access with standard card earning.

Silver/Gold Tiers: Ad-free experience, exclusive rewards, and accelerated Season Pass progression
